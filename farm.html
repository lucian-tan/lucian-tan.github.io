Re-designing a data analytics web platform for new agriculture technology needs

Overview

When reception was poor for a newly-launched consumer-facing app in Indonesia, my team was assigned to re-design the experience for customers to request for a courier to their doorstep. Collaborating with operation teams to re-design the experience based on user-derived insights, we managed to both reduce task time on the app significantly and introduce operational procedure changes that improved other capabilities.

Product preview

Receive delivery prices via the app with minimal time and effort

Request for a pick up with a few taps

Manage past orders and earnings via the app

Core product

Farm analysis and forecasting

Business need

Customers found the clunky interface discouraging for data uploads, while its outdated design weakened sales pitches and limited R&D’s ability to showcase new data visualisations.

Goal

To streamline the data input and analysis experience for users, while refreshing the interface to attract new customers.

Result

Existing customers expressed greater satisfaction with the user experience of the product and sales teams reported better impressions from new customers.

Integration

Connecting products to a shared ecosystem

Business need

The product lacked capabilities for a new target market, limiting growth opportunities, while sales, product, and R&D needed integrated solutions to generate more actionable data insights.

The products' fragmented workflows and components hindered user adoption, while technical debt in the codebase slowed engineering efficiency.

Goal

To simplify setup and input across different data types so as to generate more accurate insights with greater ease. Achieving this differentiates the product by delivering data insights with superior accuracy and real-world relevance.

Result

Self-service customers generated insights more efficiently, while consultants leveraged the system to automate tasks and reduce delivery time. Additionally, a new design system lowered development efforts.

Design outcomes

Obtain prices with less effort

With fewer steps and inputs required, minutes are shaved off the time taken to receive prices. Customers may input full parcel details if they wish to.

Getting details at the appropriate moment

Designed to follow customer habits, pick up location details are requested just when the customer is ready for it. Past address inputs are also saved to eliminate repetitive tasks.

Requesting a pick up

Pickup location setting becomes more relevant to the moment: Customers only enter their address information when they are ready
